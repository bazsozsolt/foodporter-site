Work as a rider

Timpul tău, regulile tale, câștigurile tale — your time, your rules, your earnings.

  • You are employed by us directly: no bogus self-employment.
  • Pay under the applicable collective agreement (CAO), including surcharges and pension.
  • Choose an on-call or a min-max contract.
  • An extra bonus per delivery on top of your hourly wage.
  • Tips go 100% to you.
  • Safe work: mandatory breaks and rest periods are checked in the app.

Exact amounts are in your employment contract and the rider app.
